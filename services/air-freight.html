AIR LOGISTICS / 01

AIR FREIGHT SOLUTIONS WITHOUT BORDERS

When time is critical, air freight provides the speed, flexibility and global reach required to keep your supply chain moving.

SAMA Transportations provides coordinated air cargo solutions for urgent shipments, industrial equipment, spare parts, high-value cargo and time-sensitive project requirements.

From airport-to-airport transportation to complete door-to-door logistics, we coordinate cargo collection, ground handling, airport operations, documentation, air transportation and final delivery.

For complex or oversized shipments that cannot move through conventional scheduled air cargo services, we can also coordinate dedicated charter and heavy-lift air cargo solutions according to project requirements.

Our objective is simple: to move your cargo safely, efficiently and with full operational coordination from origin to destination.
