SERVICE OVERVIEW

VEHICLE TRANSPORTATION

SAMA TRANSPORTATIONS provides safe, planned and professional vehicle transportation solutions throughout Iraq and across regional countries.

With specialized car-carrier equipment, experienced drivers and a dedicated operations team, we manage vehicle movements from the point of collection to the final destination with a strong focus on safety, operational control and timely delivery.

Our transportation solutions cover passenger cars, SUVs and 4x4 vehicles, commercial vehicles and project fleets, as well as high-volume vehicle distribution operations.

We organize the transportation of imported vehicles from ports to storage yards, vehicle stock areas, dealerships, project sites and delivery points in different cities.

The complete operation — including loading, vehicle securing, route planning, transportation monitoring, unloading and final delivery — is managed through a coordinated logistics process.

From individual vehicle movements to large-scale fleet distribution projects, SAMA TRANSPORTATIONS provides reliable, efficient and controlled vehicle logistics solutions.
