SERVICE OVERVIEW

CONTROLLED STORAGE. CONFIDENT CARGO FLOW.

SAMA Transportations provides coordinated warehousing and cargo handling support for cargo moving through Iraq. From Umm Qasr Port to strategically positioned facilities in Hilla, Baghdad, Duhok and Safwan, our teams help create a safer, more visible transition between arrival, storage and onward transport.

SERVICE CAPABILITIES

BUILT FOR EVERY STAGE OF HANDLING

  • Umm Qasr Port coordination
  • Warehousing across Iraq
  • Loading and unloading
  • Cargo handling and staging
  • Secure stacking operations
  • Heavy equipment handling
