WAREHOUSING, HANDLING & DISTRIBUTION

FROM PORT ARRIVAL
TO FINAL INLAND DELIVERY.

SAMA Transportations provides integrated warehousing, cargo handling and inland distribution solutions designed to keep cargo moving smoothly from Iraqi ports to warehouses and onward to final destinations across Iraq.

We coordinate cargo collection from ports, transportation to storage facilities, unloading, handling, temporary or long-term storage, reloading and final inland transportation according to each shipment's operational requirements.

Whether the cargo arrives through Umm Qasr, Khor Al Zubair or other Iraqi ports, our team manages the complete flow from port reception to warehouse operations and final dispatch.

Our services support bulk cargo, industrial materials, project cargo, construction materials, machinery and general commercial shipments requiring secure storage and efficient onward transportation.

    Short-term and long-term storage solutions for different cargo types.

    RELOADING & DISPATCH

    Cargo is prepared, reloaded and dispatched according to the customer's delivery schedule.

  6. 06

    INLAND TRANSPORTATION

    Cargo is transported from the warehouse to factories, project sites, distribution centers and final destinations throughout Iraq.
